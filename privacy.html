Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy covers the mobile games Kingdom Watch: Tower Defense, Orc Siege: Tower Defense, Necro Spire: Tower Defense and Moonwood: Tower Defense (the "Games"), made by Nexus671 Games.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. We have no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your game progress (gold, towers, upgrades, hearts and settings) is saved only on your device and never leaves it. Uninstalling a Game deletes this data.

Advertising

The Games show ads from Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the following to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads and the app (for example ad views, taps and app launches)
  • Diagnostic information (for example app performance and crash data)

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Games ask for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).

Children

The Games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
